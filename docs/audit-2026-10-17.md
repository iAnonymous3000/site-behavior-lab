A review of `main` at `27d944c3` (also `production`; the container reported
deployment `27d944c3` and the static site's status page named snapshot
source `27d944c3`) for evidence integrity, the measurement pipeline,

## 4. Open findings, by impact

1. **Fingerprinting evidence lost on widget pages (fixed in `2a965394`).**
   In the 500 sweep reports all 121 pages that render a Taboola widget lose
   a frame, against 1 of the other 379. The cause is the first exit: the
   widget's ellipsis code measures each character and character pair of its
   headlines, and the 128-distinct-string `measureText` bound nulled the
   main frame's whole snapshot, a real canvas and audio read on abc10.com
   included. Section 10 has the evidence, the change and what stays open.
2. **A privacy hub read as the policy.** theguardian.com's policy link
   reaches a 2,426-character hub whose full policy is elsewhere. Measured
   and fixed for four of the five hub sites found in `ac89fc6b`; what stays
   open is in section 9.
3. **Cause-less 500 for resource-limit refusals.** The two known producer
   paths are closed, and the log now names the limit, but the public answer
   is still "try again later". A declared cause is new public vocabulary.
4. **CSV formula after a semicolon (fixed, section 12).** `csvCell` neutralizes only a leading
   formula character; a crafted import can carry one after `;`. Whether a
   semicolon-delimited spreadsheet evaluates it was not demonstrated.
5. **Labels and copy.** An offscreen field the synthetic-input probe skips
   is recorded as truncated and `evidence-cap-reached`, though nothing was
   capped. The report header leads with schema and derivation terms ("v1
   schema, facts legacy-derived"), and the bottom-line card's method
   sentence runs past 100 words. The print view overflows a 375 px screen.
   The unavailable-report page has no site navigation and does not state
   the retention period.

## 10. Addendum, 2026-10-18: the Taboola frame loss (finding 1)

- **Cause.** Exit (a) of finding 1, in the main frame. An observer build
  that tags every write of its coverage latch (14 sites; 7 of 7 local
  fixtures produced their expected tag) scanned augusta.com,
  nwfdailynews.com, wdam.com and abc10.com. On each, every frame but the
  main one was readable, and the main frame's snapshot was null at the
  128-distinct-string `measureText` bound. The caller is Taboola's ellipsis
  code (`fillPolyfillEllipsis` in the gannett-network, meredith-network and
  tegna-network1 loaders under `cdn.taboola.com/libtrc/`), which measures
  each character and character pair of its headlines in the widget's fonts:
  1,482 to 4,993 calls of one- and two-character strings in 4 to 8 fonts.
  cbsnews.com, without the widget, read all 126 frames clean. The nulled
