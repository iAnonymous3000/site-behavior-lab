# Audit, 2026-10-17

A review of `main` at `27d944c3` (also `production`; the container reported
deployment `27d944c3` and the static site's status page named snapshot
source `27d944c3`) for evidence integrity, the measurement pipeline,
security, and the scan and reading journeys, followed by fixes. This record
states what was found, what changed, what was verified and how, and what is
left, so later audits do not re-file it. Section 8 records the push to
`main` that followed and what main CI found there.

## 4. Open findings, by impact

1. **Fingerprinting evidence lost on widget pages (unconfirmed defect).**
   In the 500 sweep reports the fingerprint observer records a loss on 183
   (37%). All 121 pages that render a Taboola widget lose a frame, against
   1 of the other 379, and among complete visits 3 of 121 widget pages
   record any fingerprint event or API detection, against 190 of 322 pages
   without the widget. A local fixture reproduces the
   signature two ways: a bounded-evidence overflow (more than 128 distinct
   `measureText` strings, or more than 256 distinct characters on one
   canvas) nulls the whole frame's snapshot, or a stack-integrity exit. If
   the first fires on these pages, one bound discards every fingerprint
   finding in the frame, including a real canvas read. Settle it with an
   observer build that logs which exit set coverage loss, on one such page.
2. **A privacy hub read as the policy.** theguardian.com's policy link
   reaches a 2,426-character hub whose full policy is elsewhere; reads of
   3,000 characters or more are not withheld, so its omission card stands.
3. **Cause-less 500 for resource-limit refusals.** The two known producer
   paths are closed, and the log now names the limit, but the public answer
   is still "try again later". A declared cause is new public vocabulary.
4. **CSV formula after a semicolon.** `csvCell` neutralizes only a leading
   formula character; a crafted import can carry one after `;`. Whether a
   semicolon-delimited spreadsheet evaluates it was not demonstrated.
5. **Labels and copy.** An offscreen field the synthetic-input probe skips
   is recorded as truncated and `evidence-cap-reached`, though nothing was
   capped. The report header leads with schema and derivation terms ("v1
   schema, facts legacy-derived"), and the bottom-line card's method
   sentence runs past 100 words. The print view overflows a 375 px screen.
   The unavailable-report page has no site navigation and does not state
   the retention period.

## 5. Owner decisions

- **The stopped sweep.** Every scanner commit above changes the instrument,
  so collection SHA `7e761290` no longer describes it. Restarting on a new
  SHA, and whether to include these changes, is the owner's call.
- **Corrections ledger.** The withheld short-read cards (section 2) change
  what 35 committed reports render without a ledger entry, following the
  alias-hedge precedent. Recording them as corrections is open.
- **Declared cause** for a persistence resource-limit refusal (finding 3).
- **Latency.** The policy settle wait costs up to about 3 s per scan
  (section 3); the 4 s bound is a judgment, not a measured optimum.

   not run named.

   outside the repository are confined to the audit work area.

the faithfulness pass; no screen reader was used; no representative users
