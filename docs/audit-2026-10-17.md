# Audit, 2026-10-17

A review of `main` at `27d944c3` (also `production`; the container reported
deployment `27d944c3` and the static site's status page named snapshot
source `27d944c3`) for evidence integrity, the measurement pipeline,
security, and the scan and reading journeys, followed by fixes. This record
states what was found, what changed, what was verified and how, and what is
left, so later audits do not re-file it. Nothing here was pushed or
deployed.

## 1. Method and evidence

- **Baseline.** `npm run typecheck`, `npm run cf:typecheck`, the whole unit
  suite and `npm run build` (with the documented
  `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_BEHAVIOR_LAB_SITE_URL`) passed at `27d944c3`.
- **Real-site evidence.** The restarted reliability sweep stopped on
  2026-10-03 at case 1,364 (chroniclet.com) on a cause-less
  `report-resource-limit` 500. Its local store kept 500 r2 reports from
  news sites, produced by `7e761290`, whose code is identical to
  `27d944c3` (one documentation file differs), plus 1,363 outcome rows.
  These were read, never modified.
- **Independent checks.** Claims were tested against evidence the
  implementation did not produce: reference renders and fetches of policy
  pages, recomputation of report counts from evidence rows, row dumps of
  the producer contract across commits, and local scans of real sites
  before and after each scanner change.
- **Subagents.** Two read-only investigations (capture-loss causes,
  security). Every claim acted on below was re-verified by execution here.

## 3. Verification

- **Gates.** The whole unit suite (4,287 tests, 15 stages) passed on the
  combined changes before the last commit; `npm run check` with the
  documented site URL is the final gate (section 6).
- **Regression tests fail on the parent.** Each new guard was removed or
  reverted alone, compiled and run, and its test failed: both halves of the
  deep-host fix, the code-point measurement, the five policy-visit guards
  (one test each), and the four security fixes. The policy fixtures were
  also run against `27d944c3`, where four of the five fail for the targeted
  reason.
- **Identity.** The normalization identity ledger did not move. A canonical
  dump of every producer row at `9ff0ab81` and after the detector epoch
  shows all 67 Node and 20 PageGraph rows byte-identical, with two v18 rows
  added that differ from the closed v17 pair only in the registry and the
  policy version.
- **Real sites, before and after, same machine and hour.** augusta.com and
  khanacademy.org read their full policies under both versions;
  lbpost.com reads its genuine 3,423-character policy under both;
  nwfdailynews.com and press-citizen.com (proxy-capped) move from
  failed/load-failed to skipped/budget-unavailable. A first version that
  withheld every read with an open request failed augusta.com and
  nwfdailynews.com on a DoubleClick activity fetch and chat logging calls;
  the length condition and its test came from that.
- **Faithfulness over the 500 sweep reports.** The project's own fidelity
  invariants found no failure. Independently recomputed from evidence rows:
  total, third-party and catalogued request counts, distinct third-party
  domains, cookies, third-party cookies, storage entries and summed
  fingerprint calls all match the summary when their family is complete; a
  Shields count never exceeds its phase's flags; the headline's cross-site
  request figure equals the count and carries a lower-bound mark exactly
  when requests are censored; no fingerprint absence card appears over a
  censored fingerprinting family without the incomplete marker; no failed
  run renders calm. This covers observe-mode single scans of news sites
  only.
- **Interface.** axe-core 4.13 with WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 A and AA rules
  found no violations on eleven production pages (home, directory, a
  report, a site profile, a category, methodology, glossary, catalog,
  status, corrections, an unavailable report) in desktop light, desktop
  dark and a 375 px phone layout, or on a fresh local r2 report and its
  print view. Keyboard order is logical with a skip link and visible focus.
  Locally, invalid, private and unreachable targets are announced in alert
  regions with recovery advice, focus moves to the progress region on
  submit and to the results on completion, and fresh reports offer share,
  link, CSV, JSON and PDF exports. Automated rules cover part of WCAG only;
  no assistive-technology session or user research was run.

## 4. Open findings, by impact

1. **Fingerprinting evidence lost on widget pages (unconfirmed defect).**
   In the 500 sweep reports the fingerprint observer records a loss on 183
   (37%). All 121 pages that render a Taboola widget lose a frame, against
   1 of the other 379, and among complete visits 3 of 121 widget pages
   record any fingerprint event or API detection, against 190 of 322 pages
   without the widget. A local fixture reproduces the
   signature two ways: a bounded-evidence overflow (more than 128 distinct
   `measureText` strings, or more than 256 distinct characters on one
   canvas) nulls the whole frame's snapshot, or a stack-integrity exit. If
   the first fires on these pages, one bound discards every fingerprint
   finding in the frame, including a real canvas read. Settle it with an
   observer build that logs which exit set coverage loss, on one such page.
2. **A privacy hub read as the policy.** theguardian.com's policy link
   reaches a 2,426-character hub whose full policy is elsewhere; reads of
   3,000 characters or more are not withheld, so its omission card stands.
3. **Cause-less 500 for resource-limit refusals.** The two known producer
   paths are closed, and the log now names the limit, but the public answer
   is still "try again later". A declared cause is new public vocabulary.
4. **CSV formula after a semicolon.** `csvCell` neutralizes only a leading
   formula character; a crafted import can carry one after `;`. Whether a
   semicolon-delimited spreadsheet evaluates it was not demonstrated.
5. **Labels and copy.** An offscreen field the synthetic-input probe skips
   is recorded as truncated and `evidence-cap-reached`, though nothing was
   capped. The report header leads with schema and derivation terms ("v1
   schema, facts legacy-derived"), and the bottom-line card's method
   sentence runs past 100 words. The print view overflows a 375 px screen.
   The unavailable-report page has no site navigation and does not state
   the retention period.

## 5. Owner decisions

- **The stopped sweep.** Every scanner commit above changes the instrument,
  so collection SHA `7e761290` no longer describes it. Restarting on a new
  SHA, and whether to include these changes, is the owner's call.
- **Corrections ledger.** The withheld short-read cards (section 2) change
  what 35 committed reports render without a ledger entry, following the
  alias-hedge precedent. Recording them as corrections is open.
- **Declared cause** for a persistence resource-limit refusal (finding 3).

## 6. Acceptance criteria

The work is ready for review when all of these hold at the final commit:

1. Typecheck, Cloudflare typecheck, the whole unit suite and the production
   build pass (`NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_BEHAVIOR_LAB_SITE_URL=https://example.org
   npm run check`).
2. No stored report byte changes: the 500 sweep reports read as fixed
   points and the committed corpus round-trip passes.
3. Every producer row that existed before is byte-identical; only the two
   v18 rows are added.
4. Each fix has a test that fails when its change is removed.
5. Nothing is pushed, deployed, or written outside the repository and the
   audit work area.

Validation not yet done: comparison and consent reports were not part of
the faithfulness pass; no screen reader was used; no representative users
were observed. The smallest useful experiment for the interface is a
five-task moderated session (start a scan, explain a censored family, find
a report's limits, open supporting rows, export and share) with five
non-expert participants, measuring task success and time.
