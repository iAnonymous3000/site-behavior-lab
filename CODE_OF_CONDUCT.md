# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We pledge to make participation in Site Behavior Lab a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique evidence, code, and claims rather than people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize to people affected by mistakes, and learn
  from the experience.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing personal or sensitive information.
- Prioritize the community's overall health over individual advantage.

Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, insults, trolling, doxxing, and
other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
professional setting are unacceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in repository spaces and when a participant publicly
represents the project, including issue trackers, pull requests, reviews,
discussions, and project events.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns through the repository's
[private reporting channel](https://github.com/iAnonymous3000/site-behavior-lab/security/advisories/new)
and prefix the summary with "Conduct report." This shared private intake is
intentional while the project has no separate published conduct address. Do
not disclose sensitive details in a public issue.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, commits,
issues, and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled as privately and
promptly as practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed and avoided.
